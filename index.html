GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions.

Is insurance included?

Drivers should check whether their own policy covers them to drive the campervan. If additional cover is required, temporary cover may need to be arranged separately. Get in touch and we can explain the booking requirements.

Is there a mileage limit?

The standard allowance is 100 miles per day. Planning a longer adventure? Speak to us — we can be flexible depending on the trip.

How old do I need to be?

Drivers must be aged 25 or over.

Are pets allowed?

Yes — pets are welcome in Charlie and Bazz.

What's the fuel policy?

Full to full. Collect with a full tank and return it full.

Can I leave my car when I collect the van?

Yes. Secure off-road parking behind a barrier is available while you are away.

What are collection and return times?

Standard collection is from 3pm on your first day and return is by 3pm on your final day. Alternative arrangements can be discussed beforehand.
